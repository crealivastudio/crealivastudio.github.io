Workflow

How It Works

01

Send a Brief

Tell us about the building you want visualized via WhatsApp — floor plans, site photos, or style references.

02

3D Modeling

The building is modeled in SketchUp according to the dimensions and layout you provide.

03

Enscape Rendering

The model is rendered with realistic lighting and materials, ready for promotional use.

04

Revise & Deliver

You review the result, request revisions if needed, then receive the high-resolution files.
